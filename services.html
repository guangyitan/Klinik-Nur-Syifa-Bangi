Our Medical Services

General Consultation & Treatment

Comprehensive outpatient medical care for acute and chronic conditions. Our doctors provide accurate diagnoses and tailored treatment plans for influenza, fever, infections, hypertension, and diabetes.

Walk-ins welcome

Blood Tests & Health Screening

Full laboratory analysis including lipid profiles, liver & kidney functions, blood sugar index, and customized preventative wellness screenings.

Vaccination & Immunization

Includes pediatric vaccines, seasonal flu shots, travel immunizations, and Hepatitis shots.

Lab Report Provided

Ultrasound (Scan 2D)

High-precision clinical imaging scan services supporting diagnostics and monitoring across three vital healthcare areas:

  • Pregnancy Scanning: Routine antenatal growth checkups.
  • Fast Scan (Trauma): Rapid diagnostic imaging for internal checks.
  • Gynaecology Scan: Pelvic, uterine, and ovarian diagnostics.
HD Diagnostic Imaging

Nebulization / Phlegm Suctioning

Dedicated respiratory relief services. Providing professional bronchodilator nebulization therapy for acute asthma attacks, bronchitis, and safe phlegm clearance suctioning procedures.

Instant relief care

Wound Dressing Service

Comprehensive wound care management. Safe cleaning, sterile dressing, antiseptic applications, and professional supervision for post-operative incisions, burns, and chronic ulcers.

Sterile hygiene dressing

Minor Operation

Sterile minor surgical procedures performed under local anesthesia. Our experienced doctors treat a variety of minor outpatient surgical needs:

  • Abscess (Bisul)
  • Sebaceous Cyst
  • Ganglion Cyst
  • Lipoma
  • Skin Tag
  • Wound Gap Suturing

Fast, effective, and minimally invasive laser treatment to safely eliminate skin tags. Reduces recovery times and offers clean aesthetic healing under professional supervision.
