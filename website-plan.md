# Website Plan: Klinik Nur Syifa

## Design Guidelines
- **Style:** Simple, clean, modern, and minimalistic.
- **Technologies:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully responsive design adapting to all screen sizes (mobile, tablet, and desktop).
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Primary: White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Accent/Brand: Dark Red (`#870606`)
  - Backgrounds: Very light gray (for section contrast)
  - Text: Dark Charcoal (`#333333`) for maximum readability.
- **Main Slogan:** "Your Health Is Our Concern"

## Pages Outline

### 1. Home Page (`index.html`)
- **Header/Navigation:** Logo, Navigation Links (Home, About, Services, Contact), Mobile Menu Toggle.
- **Hero Section:** Clean, welcoming headline ("Welcome to Klinik Nur Syifa Bandar Baru Bangi"), slogan, a modern clinic image, and a Call-to-Action (CTA) button ("Contact Us" / "WhatsApp Us").
- **Introduction:** A short paragraph about the clinic's dedication to providing top-quality healthcare for the family.
- **Quick Services Overview:** A grid of 3-4 key services with minimalist icons (e.g., General Consultation, Ultrasound, Health Screening).
- **Footer:** Address, contact numbers, operating hours, social media links (Facebook, Instagram), and copyright information.

### 2. About Us (`about.html`)
- **Header/Navigation**
- **Page Title:** About Us
- **Our Story / Mission & Vision:** Background of the clinic and its commitment to the Bandar Baru Bangi community.
- **Our Team (Optional):** Brief introductions to the resident doctors and medical staff.
- **Footer**

### 3. Services (`services.html`)
- **Header/Navigation**
- **Page Title:** Our Services
- **Services Details:** A clean list or card layout detailing everything the clinic offers:
  - General Consultation & Treatment
  - Blood Tests & Health Screening
    - Vaccination & Immunization
  - NebuLization / Phelgm Suctioning
  - Wound Dressing Service
  - Minor Operation (Abscess (Bisul), Sebaceous Cyst, Ganglion Cyst, Lipoma, Skin Tag, Wound Gap Suturing (Jahitan))
  - Ultrasound (Scan 2D) Pregnancy, Fast Scan (Trauma), Gynaecology scan
  - Laser Skin Tag removal
- **CTA:** "Book your consultation or appointment today."
- **Footer**

### 4. Corporate Panels (`panels.html`)
- **Header/Navigation**
- **Page Title:** Corporate Panels
- **Introduction:** A brief statement explaining that the clinic accepts various corporate panels and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) for seamless healthcare access.
- **Supported Panels:** A clean grid layout displaying logos or a structured list of accepted panels (e.g., PMCare, HealthMetrics, MiCare, RedAlert, etc.).
- **CTA:** "Is your company panel not listed? Contact us to inquire."
- **Footer**

### 5. Contact Us (`contact.html`)
- **Header/Navigation**
- **Page Title:** Get in Touch
- **Contact Information:**
  - Physical Address in Bandar Baru Bangi
  - Phone Number & WhatsApp Direct Link
- **Operating Hours:** Clear table or list of opening hours (Monday-Sunday, Public Holidays).
- **Map:** Embedded Google Map for easy navigation.
- **Social Media:** Prominent links to Facebook and Instagram.
- **Footer**
