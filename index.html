Our Services

Primary Healthcare Solutions

We offer a wide range of outpatient and diagnostic services designed to support every member of your family.

General Consultation

Expert diagnosis, treatment, and management for acute and chronic conditions, including common family illnesses.

Ultrasound Scans

Advanced 2D, 3D, 4D, and 5D ultrasound imaging for pregnancy checks, abdominal, and pelvic health screenings.

Health Screening

Comprehensive wellness blood tests, organ profiles, urine analysis, and personalized preventative screening packages.
